## Part 1: The Dashboard (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual**: Start at the Home Screen. Scroll through the "Official Deals" carousel.
- **Action**: Hover over the "10km Run" deal. Show the pulsing green status dot.
- **Narrative**: *"Welcome to TrueDeal. We're looking at a new paradigm of performance agreements where trust is code, not just a promise. Our dashboard utilizes a high-fidelity glassmorphism design to bring transparency to global performance stakes."*

## Part 2: Creation Flow (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Visual**: Click the FAB (+) button. Select "Fitness" -> "Strava".
- **Action**: Configure a rule (5km run), set the stake (R$50), and show the "Review Screen".
- **Narrative**: *"Creating an agreement is as simple as defining your meta-laws. Choose your category, link your audit channel—like Strava or X—and set your stake. Every rule is hashed and stored on the Solana blockchain."*

## Part 3: Live Tracking & Proof (1:30 - 2:15)
- **Visual**: Open an active Deal page. Show the Leaderboard and the Timeline.
- **Action**: Click on "Ver prova on-chain". Show the Solana Explorer placeholder/link.
- **Narrative**: *"Transparency is at our core. Here, you can track every participant's progress. Notice the badges for the top performers. If anyone slacks, the 'Slacker Tax' is automatically calculated and redistributed by our DealGuard engine."*

## Part 4: The Wallet (2:15 - 2:45)
- **Visual**: Go to the Wallet tab. Toggle BRL/USD/SOL.
- **Action**: Tap the "Eye" icon to hide/show balance.
- **Narrative**: *"Manage your sovereign assets with ease. Real-time conversion, privacy controls, and a clear view of your staked capital versus available liquidity. This is the financial engine of the engagement economy."*
