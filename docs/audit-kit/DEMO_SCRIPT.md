# 🎬 TrueDeal MVP Demo Script

This script is designed for the final demonstration of the TrueDeal platform, highlighting the "Sovereign Trust" architecture and the premium UX.

---

## Part 1: The Dashboard (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual**: Start at the Home Screen. Scroll through the "Official Deals" carousel.
- **Action**: Hover over a deal card in `formacao` status — show the live countdown badge ticking down to midnight GMT-3. If < 1h, show the red pulse animation.
- **Action**: Click the avatar/settings icon to open the new **ProfilePopover** — show language toggle and dark mode switch.
- **Narrative**: *"Welcome to TrueDeal. We're looking at a new paradigm of performance agreements where trust is code, not just a promise. Our dashboard utilizes a high-fidelity glassmorphism design to bring transparency to global performance stakes. Even before a deal starts, participants see a live countdown to the automatic activation at midnight Brasília time."*

## Part 2: Creation Flow (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Visual**: Click the FAB (+) button. Select "Fitness" -> "Strava".
- **Action**: Configure a rule (`km_corridos`, 5km/semana). Show the **VERIFICATION_SUBRULES** checklist that appears below the rule selector — highlight each criterion with ✅.
- **Action**: Navigate to Tela 2 (Período). Show the blue GMT-3 banner: *"O deal inicia automaticamente às 00h00 (Brasília, GMT-3)"*.
- **Action**: Set the stake (R$50), show the Review Screen. Confirm the fee is **3%** on the loser pool.
- **Narrative**: *"Creating an agreement is as simple as defining your meta-laws. Notice how the platform immediately shows you every sub-rule that will be audited—public account, minimum content length, unique posts. There's no ambiguity. The agreement starts automatically at midnight without any admin intervention."*

## Part 3: Live Tracking & Proof (1:30 - 2:15)
- **Visual**: Open an active Deal page. Show the **RULE_SUBRULES** panel and the strict-window warning banner.
- **Action**: Point to the red alert: *"1 janela perdida = eliminação permanente."*
- **Action**: Click on "Ver prova on-chain". Show the Solana Explorer link.
- **Narrative**: *"Transparency is at our core. Every participant sees exactly what will be audited—and exactly what the consequences are. One missed window means elimination, regardless of any other week's performance. This strict model, enforced by the DealGuard Engine, is what separates TrueDeal from a casual bet."*

## Part 4: The Wallet (2:15 - 2:45)
- **Visual**: Go to the Wallet tab. Toggle BRL/USD/SOL.
- **Action**: Tap the "Eye" icon to hide/show balance.
- **Narrative**: *"Manage your sovereign assets with ease. Real-time conversion, privacy controls, and a clear view of your staked capital versus available liquidity. This is the financial engine of the engagement economy."*

## Part 5: Exploration & Shakes (2:45 - 3:15)
- **Visual**: Go to the Explore tab. Perform a Daily Check-in.
- **Action**: Show the "Hall of Fame" podium.
- **Narrative**: *"TrueDeal isn't just about the stake; it's about reputation. Earn 'Shakes' through daily discipline and climb the global Hall of Fame. Your reputation is your most valuable asset in the sovereign world."*
